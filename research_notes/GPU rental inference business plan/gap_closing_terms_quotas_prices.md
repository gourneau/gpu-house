# Gap closing: provider terms, new-account quotas and live prices (read-only check, 2026-10-22)

Method: each provider's legal page, pricing page and docs were fetched directly on 2026-10-22 and quoted verbatim. Where a page could not be found or read, the entry says "not found". No accounts were created and no forms were submitted. Third-party summaries are labelled as such.

## 1. Vultr

### Takeaway
Vultr's ToS and AUP only forbid reselling Vultr's own products or "access to the Services"; they repeatedly contemplate customers serving "end users", "customers" and "downstream customers", so a public paid inference API on a Vultr GPU instance is not forbidden. The on-demand Cloud GPU list prices are MI300X $1.85, MI325X $2.00, MI355X $2.59, H100 $2.30 and B200 $3.50 per GPU-hour, all sold only as 8-GPU plans. New accounts face a manually reviewed account-limit process and two 2026 Trustpilot reviews describe a GPU approval gate.

### Inferences
- A paid public LLM API is not a resale of "Vultr's products and/or access to the Services": the API customer never touches Vultr's console, API or instances. The AUP and ToS explicitly expect "end users" and "downstream customers" and even define a "Service Provider" business model. The only friction is the 5(a) "internal business or individual purposes" license wording, which conflicts with the rest of the document and is not repeated in the 2026 AUP.
- Cloud GPU MI300X, MI325X, MI355X, B200 and H100 are sold only as 8-GPU plans on the pricing page; a single-GPU on-demand option exists only for L40S, A100 PCIe, A40 and A16.
- New accounts should expect a manual review tied to billing history; the two Trustpilot reports suggest a usage-history wait of about 30 days before larger GPU limits are granted.

### Gaps
- No published reserved or committed-use discount schedule for Vultr GPUs; "contact sales" only.
- No official Vultr document describing a GPU approval step; the only evidence is two Trustpilot reviews.
- Live stock status for MI300X, MI355X and B200 is only visible in the console after signup.
