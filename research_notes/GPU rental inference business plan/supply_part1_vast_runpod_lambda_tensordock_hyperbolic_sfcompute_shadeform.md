# GPU rental supply, part 1: Vast.ai (renter), RunPod, Lambda, TensorDock, Hyperbolic, SF Compute, Shadeform

All numbers were read live on 2026-10-22 unless a row says otherwise. "checked 2026-10-22" is abbreviated "chk 10-22". "estimate (computed)" means I multiplied a published per-GPU price by 8; the provider did not publish that node price. "not found" means I looked and could not find a public number. Third-party trackers are labeled as such. Verbatim contract language is in the final section "Terms of service quotes".

Method notes: provider pages were read with the crw scraper (JS-rendered where needed) and WebFetch. Login-walled pages (Vast console search, TensorDock dashboard, Hyperbolic app, SF Compute dashboard, Shadeform platform) could not be read; public pricing pages and docs were used instead. The Vast public search API returned HTTP 403 to an unauthenticated request.

## Key question 1: Cheapest reliable H100 SXM, H200 and B200 per GPU-hour today, on-demand and at 1, 3 and 12-month terms

### Takeaway
On-demand, the lowest published list prices on 2026-10-22 among these seven are: H100 SXM $1.73/hr "from" on Vast.ai (median $2.53, marketplace, host-set) and $2.69/hr on RunPod Community Cloud; H200 $2.25/hr "from" on Shadeform's marketplace (cloud not attributed) and $3.59/hr on RunPod Community Cloud; B200 $3.74/hr via Shadeform (Boost Run 8x node, $29.92/hr) and $5.98/hr on RunPod Community Cloud. Only Lambda publishes a fixed reserved price (16-GPU minimum, 2 weeks to 1 year: H100 $6.16, B200 $9.86 per GPU-hr, which is above its own on-demand rate). Every other 1, 3 and 12-month price is a sales call or a market quote behind login.

### Inferences
- Lambda's 1-Click Cluster price ($6.16/GPU-hr H100) is above its 8x on-demand price ($3.99). A buyer wanting a reserved 1 or 3-month block of H100s from Lambda pays more per GPU-hour than on-demand, in exchange for guaranteed 16+ GPU InfiniBand capacity. Estimate: this is not a discount product.
- For a single 8x H100 node on-demand, the cheapest fixed-price, non-marketplace quotes are RunPod Community ($21.52 computed) and, via Shadeform, Latitude ($14.34). Vast's marketplace medians ($2.53/GPU) sit below RunPod Secure ($3.99) but Vast prices are per host and change hourly.
- No provider in this set publishes a 12-month H100/H200/B200 price. All 12-month numbers require a sales conversation or an RFQ (Shadeform, 12-month minimum).

### Gaps
- Vast interruptible (bid) prices per GPU: Vast's pricing page does not label "from" as on-demand or interruptible, and the console search is login-walled. Only cloud-gpus.com's $0.93 spot reading exists, and its on-demand figure conflicts with Vast's own.
- TensorDock live prices: dashboard requires login. The only TensorDock-authored table is dated July 24, 2024.
- SF Compute live index: no public price page; dashboard and API quotes require an account.
- Shadeform's /prices "from" figures for H200 ($2.25), A100 80GB ($1.35), RTX 4090 ($0.40) and RTX 5090 ($0.65) could not be tied to a named cloud from public pages.
- Hyperbolic app prices (per instance) are behind login; only the weekly "Starting From" figures are public.

## Key question 2: Which providers permit or forbid running a public, paid inference API on rented GPUs, reselling compute, or subleasing GPUs?

### Takeaway
None of the seven explicitly says "you may run a public inference API". Three contain anti-resale clauses that bite on compute itself: TensorDock ("not for resale or use by another entity"; AUP "may not ... sell, resell, or exploit any portion of our Services"), Shadeform ("any Marketplace Offerings purchased may not be resold"; use limited to "your internal business or personal purposes"), and SF Compute (resale of unused contract time is permitted only "on the Marketplace" and only with "prior authorization from the Company"). RunPod forbids reselling credits without consent and limits "the Site" to approved commercial endeavors, but references "reseller accounts" and "your end users". Vast, Lambda and Hyperbolic restrict sublicensing of their platform/website and are silent on serving third-party traffic from the rented GPU.
